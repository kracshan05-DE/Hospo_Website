# Editing Tapro products: owner guide

You can add, change, reorder and remove Tapro products yourself. You don't
need any technical knowledge, and you can't break the website.

## Signing in

1. Open **https://www.YOUR-DOMAIN/studio** in your browser. Bookmark it.
2. Sign in with the email (or Google account) you were invited with.
   Forgot your password? Use **Forgot password** on the sign-in screen.

## Add a product

1. Click **Products** on the left, then the **+** (Create) button.
2. Fill in:
   - **Product name** (required), e.g. *Red Kochchi Sauce*
   - **Size / volume**, e.g. *250ml Bottle*
   - **Description**: a line or two about the product
   - **Photo**: drag a photo in, or click **Upload**. Any size is fine.
3. Click **Publish** (bottom right).

The website updates **about 2 minutes** after you publish.

## Change a product

Click the product, make your changes, then click **Publish**. Until you
publish, changes are saved as a draft that only you can see.

## Change the order

In the **Products** list, drag a product up or down by the handle on its left.
The website shows products in this order.

## Remove a product

Open the product, click the **⋯** menu next to Publish, then **Delete**.
To hide it temporarily instead, choose **Unpublish**.

## Made a mistake?

Open the product and click the clock icon (**History**) at the top. Choose an
earlier version and click **Restore**, then **Publish**.

## Photo tips

- Portrait photos (taller than wide) fit the website's cards best.
- After uploading, click the **crop** icon on the photo to choose which part
  must always stay visible.
- Optionally add a **photo description**: it is read aloud to blind visitors.
